# Code of Conduct

We want Byoc to be a project where everyone feels welcome to take part, whatever their experience, background, identity or abilities. This code applies to everyone involved: team members, outside contributors, reviewers and testers.

## What we expect

- **Be respectful.** Disagree with ideas, never attack people.
- **Be constructive.** In reviews and issues, explain what is wrong and suggest how to fix it.
- **Be patient with newcomers.** Every question is welcome; answer it or point to the right place in the docs.
- **Assume good intent.** A short message is usually a busy person, not a rude one. Ask before judging.
- **Own your mistakes.** Admit them, fix them, move on.
- **Think about every player.** We build for people with all kinds of abilities; accessibility remarks are always in scope.

## What we do not accept

- Insults, mockery, personal or political attacks
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private
- Discriminatory jokes or language
- Sexual language or imagery
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Repeatedly ignoring a request to stop

## Where it applies

Everywhere the project lives: the repository, issues, pull requests, reviews, the GitHub Project, team chats and demos.

## What happens next

The team looks into every report and responds fairly. Depending on how serious it is, we may:

1. Have a private conversation and ask for the behaviour to stop
2. Issue a written warning
3. Remove comments, commits or issues that break this code
4. Temporarily or permanently exclude someone from the project
