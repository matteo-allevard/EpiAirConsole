# Accessibility statement

> **Draft.** This statement will be completed after the accessibility audit (Sprint 6).

Byoc aims to be usable by as many people as possible, including people with visual, motor, hearing or cognitive disabilities.

## Target

- **Interfaces** (console, controller, catalog, profiles): [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) level AA
- **Games:** the [Game Accessibility Guidelines](https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/)
